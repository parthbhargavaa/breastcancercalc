Tumor Volume Reduction (TVR) estimation using the Magee method provides a practical way to assess how much a breast tumor has shrunk after neoadjuvant therapy. This method uses the largest tumor dimension from gross pathology and adjusts it based on the tumor cellularity.

Calculations:

  • Revised Tumor Size: Post-therapy Dimension × Percent Cellularity.
  • Volume Reduction: 100 × (1 - (Revised Size / Pre-therapy Size)³).

This approach helps pathologists and clinicians estimate treatment response quantitatively. For example, a tumor with a large post-therapy fibrotic area but low cellularity may indicate a substantial therapy effect despite the size appearing large grossly.
